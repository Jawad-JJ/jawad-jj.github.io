Privacy Policy

Salami Khata · Effective 7 November 2026

Salami Khata is a notebook for recording wedding salami and gifts that you receive and give. You can use it as a guest, or sign in so your khata is saved online. This policy explains what happens to your information in each case.

If you use the app as a guest

Everything you enter (names, amounts, events, dates and notes) is saved only on your phone. Nothing is sent to us or anyone else.

If you sign in

When you sign in with Google or with an email address, we receive your email address and your name (if you give one), and we store your khata: the entries and events you write, including the names of people, amounts, relations, dates and notes. We use this only to keep your khata safe and to show it on every phone where you sign in.

The data is stored with Google Firebase (Firebase Authentication and Cloud Firestore), which processes it for us. It is sent over encrypted connections. Security rules allow only your own signed-in account to read or change your khata. We do not sell it, share it with anyone else, or use it for advertising.

Ads, analytics and tracking

None. The app has no ads, no analytics and no tracking.

When data leaves the app

Only when you choose: for example, when you tap Share summary, Save backup file or Export spreadsheet and pick an app such as WhatsApp or Google Drive. What happens after that is covered by the policy of the app you chose.

Internet use

The app works offline. When online, it downloads its own files, Urdu fonts from Google Fonts and, if you sign in, the Firebase sign-in library. No personal information is included in these downloads.

Keeping and deleting your data

Your online khata is kept until you delete it. You can delete single entries, delete all entries, or delete your whole account from Settings in the app. Deleting your account removes your sign-in record and every entry and event from our database straight away. You can also ask us to delete your account. Guests can remove their data with Settings → Delete all entries or by uninstalling the app.

Children

The app is meant for adults managing family events and is not directed at children.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date. For questions, contact the developer at the email address shown on the app's Google Play listing.
