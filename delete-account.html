Delete your account

Salami Khata · Effective 7 November 2026

You can delete your Salami Khata account, and every entry and event saved in it, at any time.

Delete it inside the app (fastest)

  1. Open Salami Khata and go to Settings.
  2. Under Account, tap Delete my account.
  3. Confirm with your Google account or your password.

Your account and all of its entries and events are deleted from our database straight away, and removed from your phone.

Ask us to delete it

If you no longer have the app, email the developer at the address shown on the app's Google Play listing, from the email address you signed in with, with the subject Delete my Salami Khata account. We delete the account and all of its data within 30 days and reply to confirm.

What is deleted

Your sign-in record (email address and name) and every entry and event in your khata. Nothing is kept afterwards. Backup files you saved yourself, for example in WhatsApp or Google Drive, are not touched; delete them there if you want them gone.
